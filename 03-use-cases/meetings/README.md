# Meetings

## Prepare a meeting brief

> Prepare me for a `[meeting type]` with `[participants/roles]` about `[topic]`. Use only the notes and documents supplied. Return: purpose, desired decisions, current position, important facts, open questions, risks and a short agenda. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions. Keep it to one page.

## Turn notes into minutes and actions

> Convert these meeting notes or transcript excerpts into concise minutes. Include: summary, decisions explicitly made, actions (action / owner / due date), risks or dependencies, and unresolved questions. Use `Not stated` where owner or date is missing. Preserve dissent and uncertainty. Do not invent consensus or decisions.

## Draft a follow-up

> Draft a follow-up message for attendees based on the approved minutes below. Highlight decisions and actions, clearly identify unassigned items, and ask owners to correct inaccuracies by `[date]`. Do not imply that proposed actions have been accepted if they were not agreed.

## Find meeting patterns

> Review these meeting summaries for recurring blockers, repeated decisions, unresolved topics and actions that regularly slip. Cite the meeting/date for each pattern. Distinguish a repeated observation from a root-cause conclusion.

**Human check:** transcript accuracy, speaker attribution, decision status, action owners, due dates and whether sensitive discussion belongs in circulated minutes.

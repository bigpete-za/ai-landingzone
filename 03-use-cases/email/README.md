# Email

## Draft a new email

> Draft an email to `[recipient/audience]` about `[topic]`. The outcome I need is `[desired response or action]`. Context: `[facts and background]`. Use a `[tone]` tone, keep it under `[word count]` words, put the key message first and make the next step explicit. Do not invent commitments, dates or approvals. Include a subject line and mark anything I need to confirm in `[square brackets]`.

## Reply to a thread

> Using only the email thread below, draft a reply that acknowledges the sender's main points, answers the questions we can answer, and lists any items that need confirmation. Keep the tone `[tone]`. Do not imply that we agree to a request unless the thread or my instructions explicitly authorise it. Flag risks or commitments before the draft.

## Rewrite for clarity

> Rewrite this email for `[audience]` so it is `[clearer / shorter / warmer / firmer]`. Preserve the meaning, facts and commitments. Do not add new information. Return the revised email followed by a short list of material changes.

## Triage an inbox export

> Categorise these messages by topic, urgency and required action. Return sender, subject, date, summary, proposed next action and reason for priority. Do not infer urgency from tone alone. Flag messages requiring a human decision. Do not send, delete, archive or forward anything.

**Human check:** recipient, names, attachment, tone, confidentiality, dates, commercial/legal commitments and whether the draft is actually authorised.

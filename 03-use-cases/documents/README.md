# Documents and Writing

## Create a first draft

> Draft a `[document type]` for `[audience]` to achieve `[purpose]`. Use this outline: `[outline]`. Use only `[approved source material]` for facts. Style: `[plain language / formal / concise]`. Include placeholders for missing facts and a short list of questions the author must resolve. Do not present the draft as approved policy or legal wording.

## Review a procedure

> Review this procedure for clarity, sequence, missing prerequisites, ambiguous roles, unsafe assumptions and points where a user could make an error. Return findings ranked by impact, with section references and suggested corrections. Do not invent technical or policy requirements.

## Evaluate against a rubric

> Evaluate this draft against the supplied rubric. For each criterion, give the rating, the exact passage or evidence supporting it, any gap, and a specific improvement. Use only the rubric's stated scale and definitions. Mark criteria that cannot be assessed from the draft as `Insufficient evidence`; do not guess a score. Separate factual accuracy, policy compliance and completeness from style or readability. Finish with the highest-priority revisions.

For a more repeatable review procedure, see [Document Review](../../05-skills-and-workflows/document-review/SKILL.md). AI rubric scores are screening signals, not independent proof of correctness or compliance.

## Compare document versions

> Compare Version A and Version B. Identify substantive changes to meaning, scope, obligations, dates, figures and responsibilities separately from formatting or style. Quote the relevant section headings and state whether a change may require owner review. Do not infer intent.

## Simplify for a reader

> Rewrite this content for `[reader knowledge level]`. Preserve technical accuracy and important caveats. Define unavoidable jargon and use short sentences. Then list any detail you simplified or removed.

**Human check:** factual accuracy, policy authority, document owner, version, approvals and legal obligations.

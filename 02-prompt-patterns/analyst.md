# Analyst Pattern

**Purpose:** inspect supplied information, identify patterns and compare options without presenting assumptions as facts.

**Prompt**

> Act as a neutral business analyst. Analyse `[question or decision]` using only `[named sources/data]`.
>
> First describe the data or evidence available, its scope and any limitations. Then:
> - identify the main findings and supporting evidence;
> - distinguish fact, calculation, interpretation and assumption;
> - compare relevant options against `[criteria]`;
> - identify anomalies, gaps, conflicting evidence and possible bias;
> - give a balanced conclusion and the next information needed.
>
> Do not fill missing data with guesses. Cite the source or show the calculation for each material finding. If the evidence does not support a conclusion, say so.

**Useful follow-ups**

- Reanalyse using a different weighting of the criteria.
- Give the strongest counterargument to the leading option.
- Turn the findings into a decision table for `[audience]`.

**Verify:** formulas, source completeness, sample size, units, dates and whether the analysis answers the actual decision.

# Critic Pattern

**Purpose:** improve a draft by finding weaknesses before it is shared.

**Prompt**

> Review the following `[document/email/report/plan/presentation]` as a constructive but rigorous reviewer.
>
> Audience and purpose: `[audience and intended outcome]`.
> Review criteria: `[accuracy, logic, completeness, clarity, tone, risk, structure]`.
> Source of truth: `[reference material or policy]`.
>
> Do not rewrite it immediately. First provide:
> 1. The most important issues ranked High / Medium / Low.
> 2. The exact passage or section affected.
> 3. Why it is an issue and what evidence supports the concern.
> 4. A practical correction.
> 5. Questions that must be answered by the author.
>
> Distinguish objective errors from stylistic preferences. Do not invent policy or facts. Then offer a revised draft only after the review.

**Useful follow-ups**

- Apply only the high-priority corrections.
- Review from the perspective of a sceptical customer / executive / auditor.
- Check for unsupported claims, hidden assumptions and unintended commitments.

**Verify:** the reviewer has the right source documents and is not introducing unsupported edits.

# Summarizer Pattern

**Purpose:** compress material while retaining what matters for a defined audience.

**Prompt**

> Summarise `[source material]` for `[audience]` who need to `[purpose]`.
>
> Use this structure:
> - Executive summary: `[length]`.
> - Key points: no more than `[number]`.
> - Decisions made and by whom (only if explicitly stated).
> - Actions: task, owner and due date (use `Unassigned` / `Not stated` when absent).
> - Risks, dependencies and unresolved questions.
> - Source references for important claims.
>
> Preserve important qualifications and disagreement. Do not infer agreement, invent owners or dates, or add facts from outside the source.

**Useful follow-ups**

- Produce a 30-second verbal briefing.
- Create a version for an executive / customer / new starter.
- List what was omitted from the summary and why.

**Verify:** omissions, speaker attribution, action ownership, dates, numbers and tone.

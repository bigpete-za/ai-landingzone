# Coach Pattern

**Purpose:** help someone learn, prepare or improve through guided practice rather than just giving an answer.

**Prompt**

> Act as a patient, practical coach helping me improve at `[skill or topic]` for `[work context]`.
>
> My current level: `[beginner / some experience / confident]`.
> My goal: `[specific outcome]`.
> Constraints: `[time, tools, preferred learning style]`.
>
> Start by asking up to three questions if needed. Then teach one concept at a time using a workplace example. Give me a short exercise, wait for my answer, and provide specific feedback. Correct misunderstandings clearly and distinguish established facts from simplifications. Finish with a short recap and one next exercise.

**Useful follow-ups**

- Explain that again using a simpler or more technical example.
- Give me a realistic scenario and assess my response.
- Turn the topic into a 20-minute learning plan.

**Verify:** technical details, policy statements and any certification or regulatory claims against current authoritative sources.

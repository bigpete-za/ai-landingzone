# AI Safe-use — Quick Checklist

Before you use AI:

- [ ] Is this an organisation-approved AI tool and account?
- [ ] Is the data classification permitted for this tool and workflow?
- [ ] Am I allowed to access and share every source I provide?
- [ ] Have I removed unnecessary personal, customer or confidential details?
- [ ] Have I stated the task, context, source material and expected output?
- [ ] Have I told the model what not to assume or do?

Before you use the output:

- [ ] Have I checked important facts against authoritative sources?
- [ ] Have I checked citations, quotes, dates, calculations and names?
- [ ] Have I looked for missing context, bias and unsupported conclusions?
- [ ] Have I reviewed tone, audience fit and unintended commitments?
- [ ] Does a qualified person need to approve it?
- [ ] Am I authorised to send, publish or act on it?

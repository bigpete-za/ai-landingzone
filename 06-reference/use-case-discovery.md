# Use-case Discovery Worksheet

Use a short workshop or team conversation to identify where AI might help. Start with the work, not with a feature demo.

## Questions

1. Which tasks are repeated weekly or monthly?
2. Which tasks involve drafting, summarising, searching, comparing, classifying or reformatting information?
3. Where do people spend time moving information between documents, spreadsheets, email and meetings?
4. What inputs and outputs are involved?
5. Which sources are authoritative, and who owns them?
6. What information sensitivity or permission issues exist?
7. What happens if the output is wrong?
8. Who must review or approve the result?
9. How will you measure improvement?

## Prioritisation

Score each idea from 1 (low) to 5 (high):

| Factor | High score means |
|---|---|
| Frequency | Happens often |
| Time/effort | Meaningful current effort |
| Standardisation | Clear inputs and repeatable output |
| Reviewability | Easy for a person to validate |
| Business value | Measurable benefit to quality, speed or service |
| Risk/manageability | Low consequence or strong existing controls |

Choose a small first set where value is high and risk is manageable. A high-impact task is not automatically a good first use case.

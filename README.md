# AI Landing Zone

A practical toolkit for using AI effectively and responsibly at work. Designed for knowledge workers in small and medium-sized organisations, it brings together foundational guidance, reusable prompts, task examples, repeatable workflows and curated resources.

This is a **working library**, not a machine-learning course or developer prompt collection. Use what fits your task and adapt it to your tools, data and workplace.

## Start here

1. **Get the basics:** [AI mindset](01-foundations/ai-mindset.md) and [safe use](01-foundations/safe-use.md).
2. **Shape your request:** [Prompt planning](01-foundations/planning.md) and [prompt patterns](02-prompt-patterns/README.md).
3. **Try a real task:** browse [use cases](03-use-cases/README.md).
4. **Using Microsoft 365?** See [Which Copilot Tool When?](04-copilot-guides/which-tool-when.md).
5. **Make recurring work repeatable:** explore [skills and workflows](05-skills-and-workflows/README.md).

> **Need a more rigorous check?** Use [Formal Verification Mode](02-prompt-patterns/formal-verification-mode.md) or explicitly invoke the [Formal Verification skill](05-skills-and-workflows/formal-verification/SKILL.md). It is opt-in, not an automatic mode for ordinary research or critique.

## A few useful principles

- **Start with the task.** Define what you need, the context available and what a useful result looks like.
- **Keep people accountable.** Review anything you plan to send, publish, approve or act on.
- **Use approved tools and data.** Follow workplace policy and avoid entering sensitive information into unapproved services.
- **Ground and verify important work.** Provide relevant source material; check claims, calculations and assumptions in proportion to the stakes.
- **Treat outputs as drafts.** Refine the result, make uncertainty visible and turn successful approaches into repeatable workflows.

## Using the library

Choose a prompt pattern for a general thinking task, a use case for a specific activity, or a skill for a repeatable procedure. Replace placeholders, provide the sources the model is allowed to use, and review the result before relying on it. Product capabilities, connectors, privacy terms and licensing vary, so check the guidance relevant to your environment.

## Content and contributions

This is an evolving collection, not a guarantee that every prompt will produce a correct result. Contributions should be focused, safe to share and easy to test, with clear purpose, prerequisites, expected output and verification steps. For product-specific guidance, prefer official documentation and review it as products change.

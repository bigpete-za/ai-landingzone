# Microsoft 365 Copilot — Safe-use Checklist

Before using Copilot with work information:

- [ ] Confirm the account is the organisation-approved work account.
- [ ] Confirm the specific Copilot experience and its data-protection terms.
- [ ] Confirm the information is permitted for that service and workflow.
- [ ] Check access to relevant SharePoint sites, Teams, mailboxes and files.
- [ ] Avoid uploading or referencing content you are not authorised to use.
- [ ] Treat AI output and retrieved content as untrusted until checked.
- [ ] Verify citations and open the original source for important claims.
- [ ] Review drafts before sending; do not permit automatic external actions without explicit approval.
- [ ] Report accidental exposure or unexpected data access promptly.

For administrators and rollout owners, see [Microsoft's Copilot adoption guidance](https://adoption.microsoft.com/en-us/Copilot/) and [Copilot privacy and security documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/deployoffice/privacy/microsoft-365-copilot).

# Which Copilot Tool When?

Choose the experience that fits the **work you need done, the context it needs, and how much action it should take**. The newest or most capable tool is not always the right one.

## Quick choice

| If you need to… | Start with… | Why / watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| Ask a question, brainstorm, explain a concept, or get help with a short task | **Copilot Chat** | Best for interactive back-and-forth and general assistance. Be clear whether you want web research, supplied sources, or work-grounded answers. |
| Research a difficult question and produce a sourced report | **Researcher** or Chat with a research brief | Researcher is designed for deeper, multi-source research. Check citations, source quality, dates and gaps. |
| Analyse data, test a hypothesis, or explain trends in a workbook | **Excel with Copilot** or **Analyst** | Prefer Excel when the workbook is the working artefact. Validate formulas, ranges, assumptions and calculations. |
| Draft or revise a document or presentation | **Word or PowerPoint with Copilot** | Best when working directly in an Office file. Review the narrative, facts, layout, charts and source material. |
| Work with email, meetings, chats or a calendar | **Outlook or Teams with Copilot** | Use the app when its thread, meeting or communication context matters. Check permissions, transcript availability, recipients and commitments. |
| Delegate a task that spans several apps, sources and deliverables | **Copilot Cowork** | Best for multi-step work such as preparing a meeting pack from email, Teams and files, coordinating follow-ups, or researching and producing a report and deck. It plans and carries out work, not just returns advice. |
| Automate a narrow, stable task with defined inputs and outputs | **An approved agent, skill or workflow** | Use a specialised, repeatable solution when the scope and rules are clear. Define access, exception handling, testing and human approval. |
| Perform an action in a connected business system | **An approved agent or Cowork plugin** | Check the connector, data scope, publisher, permissions and whether actions are read-only or write-capable. |

## Copilot Cowork: when to use it

**Copilot Cowork is for delegation.** Describe the outcome you want, give it relevant context and constraints, then follow its plan as it works across Microsoft 365. Depending on availability and configuration, it can search organisational content, conduct multi-source research, create or edit Office files, prepare briefings, manage calendar tasks, draft or send communications, post in Teams, and run scheduled or event-triggered tasks.

Good candidates are tasks with several connected steps and a tangible result:

- **Prepare a meeting:** gather relevant email, Teams conversations and documents; create a briefing; propose an agenda and follow-up.
- **Produce a deliverable:** research a topic, synthesise approved sources, and create a report, spreadsheet or presentation.
- **Coordinate work:** draft a project plan, identify owners and open questions, prepare status communications, and organise supporting files.
- **Manage recurring work:** create a scheduled briefing or report, or—where enabled—an event-driven task triggered by a matching email or Teams message.

### A practical way to delegate

Give Cowork a clear brief. Specify:

1. **Outcome:** what should be true when the task is finished?
2. **Scope and sources:** which mailbox, files, dates, teams, folders or external sources should it use—and what is out of scope?
3. **Deliverables:** the expected files, summaries, messages or calendar changes.
4. **Constraints:** audience, tone, deadline, format, exclusions and any policy requirements.
5. **Approval boundary:** what may it prepare, and what must it show you before sending, sharing, scheduling or changing anything?

Example:

> Prepare a briefing for tomorrow’s project review. Use the latest project files in SharePoint and relevant messages and meeting notes from the last 30 days. Summarise progress, decisions, risks, blockers and unresolved questions; cite or identify the source for each key point. Create a concise Word briefing and a draft Teams update. Do not send or post anything, change files, or schedule meetings until I review and approve the proposed actions. Flag missing or conflicting information rather than guessing.

For longer tasks, inspect the plan and progress, correct the direction early, and review the outputs and proposed actions before approving them. Use pause or cancel if the task is heading somewhere unexpected.

### What has changed recently?

Microsoft’s Cowork experience is evolving quickly. As of November 2026, current first-party documentation describes:

- **Projects** to keep related tasks, instructions, context and outputs together.
- **Skills and plugins** to extend workflows; custom skills can be created, uploaded and shared, subject to tenant availability and controls.
- **Editing existing Office files** in OneDrive and SharePoint while preserving the shared file and its version history.
- **Local browser use in Microsoft Edge** under existing sign-ins and organisational policies.
- **Scheduled and event-driven tasks** for recurring work or selected email and Teams triggers.
- **Usage-based billing and administrative controls**, including visibility and spending limits.
- **A changing model selector and rollout of a new Copilot Home experience** that brings Chat and Cowork together. Some new experiences may be Frontier or otherwise limited to eligible tenants.

Feature availability, model options, billing, account eligibility and admin settings can change. Check the current [Cowork overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/cowork), [What's new](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/whats-new) and [getting-started requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/get-started) rather than assuming a feature is enabled in your tenant.

## Quick decision path

1. **Need an answer or a thinking partner?** Start with Copilot Chat.
2. **Need to work directly on an open file or in a specific app?** Use that app’s Copilot experience.
3. **Need deeper multi-source research?** Consider Researcher; specify scope and check its sources.
4. **Need coordinated work across multiple apps, steps or outputs?** Consider Cowork.
5. **Need the same bounded process repeated reliably?** Consider a tested agent, skill or workflow.
6. **Will it affect other people, records or business systems?** Set explicit approval boundaries and review every consequential action.

## Safety, permissions and practical limits

- **Approval is not verification.** Cowork can pause for approval before sensitive actions, but you still need to check the content, recipient, date, destination and consequences.
- **Keep the task bounded.** Broad instructions and complex tasks with many dependencies are more likely to be misinterpreted or only partly completed. Break high-stakes work into reviewable stages.
- **Permissions still matter.** Copilot uses the user’s available access; it does not fix oversharing, stale permissions or poorly governed content. Review SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams and mailbox access before enabling broader use.
- **Confirm the source coverage.** A plausible answer does not prove every relevant document or message was found. Ask what sources were used, what could not be accessed, and what remains uncertain.
- **Check connected tools and skills.** Custom skills are instructions, not guarantees of quality. Plugins and connectors may be provided by third parties; review publisher, permissions, actions and outputs.
- **Treat generated content as a draft.** Independently verify important claims, calculations and summaries. Use extra controls for confidential, regulated, financial, HR, legal or otherwise consequential work; do not delegate irreversible decisions without an appropriate process.
- **Plan for cost and governance.** Cowork may use usage-based billing in addition to licensing. Admins should confirm eligibility, model and plugin settings, data-protection controls, logging, cost limits and pilot scope before broad rollout.
- **Check model and data-processing terms.** Cowork can offer multiple model providers, and availability or processing conditions may differ by model and tenant configuration. Follow current organisational policy and Microsoft’s documentation for enabled models and subprocessors.

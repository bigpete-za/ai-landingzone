# Meeting to Actions

## Use when

A user needs a consistent record and follow-up from a meeting.

## Required inputs

- Meeting title and date.
- Approved notes or transcript excerpt.
- Intended audience for the minutes.
- Optional format, owner conventions and due-date rules.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the source, meeting date and intended audience.
2. Read the material and identify topics, explicit decisions, proposed ideas, questions and actions.
3. Separate decisions from discussion. Do not infer consensus.
4. Extract actions. Capture owner and due date only when explicitly stated; otherwise use `Unassigned` or `Not stated`.
5. Preserve disagreement, dependencies and unresolved questions.
6. Check names, dates and quotations against the source.
7. Draft minutes and a separate follow-up message.
8. Ask the user to review before circulation. Do not send or publish.

## Output

- Meeting details
- Summary (up to five bullets)
- Decisions explicitly made
- Action register: action | owner | due date | source reference
- Risks/dependencies
- Open questions
- Draft follow-up message

## Failure behaviour

If the source is incomplete or ambiguous, identify the gaps. Never fabricate speaker attribution, ownership, dates or decisions.

## Human approval

The meeting owner must approve the minutes and follow-up before distribution, especially where discussion is sensitive or commercially significant.

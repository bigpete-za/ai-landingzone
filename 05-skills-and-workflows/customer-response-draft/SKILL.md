# Customer Response Draft

## Use when

A team member needs to respond to a customer query, complaint or status request.

## Required inputs

- Customer message and relevant conversation history.
- Approved facts, policy or case notes.
- Desired outcome and tone.
- Any commitments or remedies already authorised.

## Procedure

1. Identify the customer's issue, request, impact and explicit questions.
2. Summarise what is known and what remains unconfirmed.
3. Check the approved policy or case facts supplied.
4. Draft an empathetic, clear response that addresses each point.
5. Separate confirmed commitments from proposed actions requiring approval.
6. Mark placeholders for missing facts and avoid speculation.
7. Provide a short internal note listing approvals or follow-up needed.

## Output

- Customer-ready draft
- Subject line if needed
- Internal verification / approval notes
- Unanswered questions and proposed next step

## Failure behaviour

Do not admit liability, promise refunds, discounts, delivery dates or remedies unless the user supplies explicit authorisation. Escalate legal threats, safety issues, personal-data incidents and serious complaints to the appropriate owner.

## Human approval

An authorised employee verifies the facts, tone and commitments before sending.

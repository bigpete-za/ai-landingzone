# Document Review

## Use when

A draft needs a structured quality review before approval or publication.

## Required inputs

- Document or draft.
- Purpose and intended audience.
- Relevant policy, source or style guide.
- Review depth and any exclusions.

## Procedure

1. Confirm version, purpose, audience and source materials.
2. Check the document structure and whether the intended outcome is clear.
3. Review factual claims against supplied sources.
4. Identify ambiguity, missing prerequisites, unsupported claims, contradictions and tone issues.
5. Rank issues by impact and identify the affected section.
6. Suggest a correction without silently changing policy or obligations.
7. Produce a revised draft only when requested.

## Output

- Overall assessment
- High / Medium / Low findings with section references
- Evidence/source mismatch register
- Questions for the document owner
- Optional revised draft with material changes identified

## Failure behaviour

Do not infer legal compliance or policy approval. Mark unsupported statements and missing evidence.

## Human approval

The content owner and any required subject-matter, legal, HR or compliance reviewer approve the final version.

# Presentation Builder

## Use when

A user needs a narrative, slide outline or speaker notes based on approved information.

## Required inputs

- Audience, purpose and desired outcome.
- Presentation duration and approximate slide count.
- Approved source document(s).
- Brand, accessibility and confidentiality requirements.

## Procedure

1. Confirm audience, purpose, time and source boundaries.
2. Extract the core message, evidence, recommendation and caveats.
3. Propose a narrative arc before creating slides.
4. Create message-led slide titles and one central idea per slide.
5. Suggest a chart, diagram or visual only where it improves understanding.
6. Identify data, claims or images requiring verification or permission.
7. Draft concise speaker notes and transitions.
8. Review flow, density, accessibility and timing.

## Output

- Storyline / agenda
- Slide-by-slide title, key message, evidence and visual suggestion
- Speaker notes
- Appendix items
- Verification / approval checklist

## Failure behaviour

Do not invent metrics, customer stories, quotations, endorsements or imagery rights.

## Human approval

The presenter owns the final narrative, data, design and publication decision.
